SPS Attendance

Native app for the Sialkot Public School & College Daily Student Checking System (attendance.spscsialkot.com). This is a clickable preview of the app's screens — the real app is Android-native (Expo) and signs into the live school system.

  • • Login — signs into the live PHP site (session cookie)
  • • Home — role-aware dashboard: admin sees all 23 classes, teachers see their class
  • • Daily Entry — uniform · shoes · socks · haircut · nails · bag checklist
  • • Notify Parents — WhatsApp alerts for non-compliant students after save
  • • Reports — daily / monthly / individual student
  • • Audit Trail, Manage, Export — admin-only tools
